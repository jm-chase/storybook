### B-9. Print productization — build items DONE 2026-07-07 (interior/cover/Lulu client); remaining needs are ACCOUNT-side: Lulu credentials + pod-package pick + public URL
The bleed PDF is POD-ready per page, but a sellable printed book needs four decisions/gaps closed:

- **Trim size:** we render 8in square; partners commonly offer 8.5in square — pick per partner and retarget `TRIM` (one constant).
- **Unit economics sanity:** ~$12–17 print cost for a 24-page square hardcover + shipping vs the $30–45 retail personalized-book incumbents charge (Wonderbly et al.) → healthy margin plus our ~$1–3 generation cost.
